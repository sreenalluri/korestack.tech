Care with a compass

True North opened in 2014 with a simple conviction: chiropractic works best when it’s honest — measured plans, gentle hands, and a finish line you can see.

Our story

Why “True North”?

Every spine has a true north — the alignment where everything moves the way it was built to. Twelve years ago, Dr. Elena Vance opened this practice after watching too many patients get trapped in endless-visit plans that treated symptoms and billed forever.

Our promise is different: we measure what’s wrong, we tell you plainly, we fix what chiropractic can fix — and we tell you honestly when it can’t. Most of our patients graduate. Many come back years later with something new, because they trust that we’ll never keep them longer than they need.

Today our team of three chiropractors and two licensed massage therapists cares for everyone from desk-bound developers to marathoners to grandparents who want to lift their grandkids without a second thought.
