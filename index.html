Your website is your
first impression.

Most customers meet your business online before they ever call or walk in. If your site looks dated, loads slowly, or breaks on a phone, they don't give you a second chance — they quietly pick the competitor who looks ready.

Why it matters

An outdated site
quietly costs you
customers.

You never meet the people who found you online, formed an opinion in seconds, and left. A modern site turns that silent leak into calls, bookings, and orders.

First impressions are digital now
Stanford research found 75% of people judge a business's credibility by its website design alone — before they read a single word about what you do.
Speed decides sales
Google found 53% of mobile visitors abandon a page that takes over 3 seconds to load. Every extra second is a customer walking out of a store you paid to open.
Google ranks modern sites first
Mobile-friendliness, HTTPS, and page speed are ranking signals. An outdated site doesn't just look old — it gets buried under competitors who invested.
Trust converts
"Not secure" warnings, a 2019 copyright footer, and broken pages tell customers nobody's home. Current info and a secure connection tell them you're open for business.
